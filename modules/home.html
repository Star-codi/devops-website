What is DevOps?

DevOps combines Development (writing software) and Operations (running it). Teams that practice DevOps ship faster, break less, and recover quicker when things go wrong.

Before DevOps, developers wrote code and threw it over the wall to operations. Deployments were scary weekend events. Bugs were found in production. DevOps fixes this by automating everything and making collaboration the default.

🍕 Real-World Analogy

Think of a pizza restaurant. The chef (developer) makes the pizza, the delivery team (operations) delivers it. Old-school IT: chef hands pizza to delivery — wrong address, cold, broken. DevOps: shared systems, automated processes, clear communication. Every pizza delivered hot, on time, every time.
